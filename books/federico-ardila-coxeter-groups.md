# Federico Ardila — Coxeter Groups (2008)

42 recorded lectures from a course taught jointly at San Francisco State University and Universidad de los Andes.

- **[Watch all lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL-XzhVrXIVeSVcV9iRJ4S9WAH1ryq4hTQ)**
- **[Ardila's lecture-by-lecture VIDEO INDEX (PDF)](https://fardila.com/Clase/coxeterindex.pdf)** — direct individual video links, topics, and durations.
- [Original course page and notes](https://fardila.com/Clase/Coxeter/lectures.html)
- [Books and additional references](https://fardila.com/Clase/Coxeter/texts.html)

To find a particular YouTube lecture, open Ardila's linked video index above. The table below mirrors the 42 lecture numbers and adds direct links to the written lecture notes; it does **not** invent individual video URLs.

For the kaleidoscope work, start with **1–4** (reflection generators), **19–24** (geometric representations and chambers), **30–35** (crystallographic root systems), and **40–42** (classification and regular polytopes).
